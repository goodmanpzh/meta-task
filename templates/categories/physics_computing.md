### Category: Physics Computing and Simulation

Tasks involving numerical physics simulations, computational physics algorithms, and scientific modeling in physics domains.

**The examples below are for inspiration only. Be creative and design your own unique task!**

**Task Pattern Ideas**:
- Quantum mechanics simulations (tunneling, wave functions, Schrödinger equation)
- Statistical mechanics (Ising model, Monte Carlo methods, Metropolis algorithm)
- Cold atom physics (microwave shielding, effective potentials)
- Numerical methods for physics (Numerov method, Runge-Kutta, finite differences)
- Thermodynamic simulations (heat transfer, phase transitions)
- Electromagnetic simulations (Maxwell equations, wave propagation)
- Particle dynamics and N-body simulations
- Computational fluid dynamics basics
- Optics simulations (ray tracing, interference patterns)
- Nuclear physics calculations (decay chains, cross-sections)

**Skills & Tools** (tag suggestions for your task):
- Core: `physics`, `numerical-methods`, `simulation`, `scientific-computing`
- Methods: `monte-carlo`, `finite-difference`, `numerov`, `runge-kutta`, `bethe-ansatz`
- Domains: `quantum-mechanics`, `statistical-mechanics`, `thermodynamics`, `electromagnetism`
- Languages: `python`, `fortran`, `c`, `julia`
- Libraries: `numpy`, `scipy`, `matplotlib`

**Possible Input Data** (be creative with environment files!):
- Physical constants and parameters (JSON/YAML configs)
- Initial conditions for simulations
- Potential energy functions (Python code)
- Experimental data for validation
- Lattice configurations for statistical mechanics
- Wave function boundary conditions
- Material properties databases
- Reference solutions for verification

**Verification Approaches**:
- Compare numerical results with analytical solutions (within tolerance)
- Verify conservation laws (energy, probability, momentum)
- Check physical constraints (T + R = 1 for tunneling)
- Statistical validation (mean, variance matching)
- Convergence tests with different grid sizes
- Comparison with published results

**Remember**: Physics computing tasks should test understanding of both the physics and the numerical methods. Include realistic physical parameters and meaningful error tolerances!
