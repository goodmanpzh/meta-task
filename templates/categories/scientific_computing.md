### Category: Scientific Computing and Data Science

Tasks involving mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, simulations, and domain-specific scientific problems.

**The examples below are for inspiration only. Be creative and design your own unique task!**

**Task Pattern Ideas**:
- Bayesian inference and probabilistic modeling (MCMC sampling)
- Time series analysis and forecasting
- Monte Carlo simulations and sampling methods
- Numerical optimization problems
- Statistical hypothesis testing
- Curve fitting and regression analysis (spectroscopy, chromatography)
- Signal processing and spectral analysis
- Network/graph analysis
- Bioinformatics: DNA/RNA primer design, sequence assembly
- Protein engineering and structure analysis
- Physics simulations and parameter tuning
- Cross-language statistical code conversion (R to Python, RStan to PyStan)

**Skills & Tools** (tag suggestions for your task):
- Core: `scientific-computing`, `data-science`, `statistics`
- Tools: `numpy`, `scipy`, `stan`, `pystan`, `rstan`, `pymc`
- Languages: `python`, `R`, `julia`
- Domains: `biology`, `physics`, `chemistry`, `bioinformatics`

**Possible Input Data** (be creative with environment files!):
- Spectroscopy data (Raman, IR, NMR spectra as `.dat`, `.csv`)
- Genomic sequences (FASTA files, GenBank `.gb`, plasmid maps)
- Protein structures (PDB files, SMILES strings, gBlock sequences)
- Time series (sensor readings, financial data, climate measurements)
- Stan/PyMC models (probabilistic model definitions, `.stan` files)
- Physics simulations (MJCF/URDF files for robotics, MuJoCo configs)
- Astronomical data (FITS files, light curves)
- Bayesian network data (CSV with observational data, DAG structures)
- Primer design specifications (target sequences, Tm requirements)
- Statistical datasets for hypothesis testing

**Verification Approaches**:
- Statistical tests (KS test, chi-square, t-test)
- Numerical tolerance checks (relative/absolute error within threshold)
- Posterior mean/std verification for Bayesian models
- Curve fitting parameter validation (peak positions, widths)
- Primer Tm and length constraints
- Sequence assembly correctness

**Remember**: These are just starting points. Design scientific challenges that require both coding skills and domain understanding!
